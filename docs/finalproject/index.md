### Spiral Design Process

The spiral design is a four step repeating process that consists of 1. Planning/Analysis, 2. Risk Evaluation, 3. Development and Testing, 4. Review and Planning Next Iteration. This process will be used in my project to help me focus in on one issue per iteration. For example, I might start with just designing for the area the shield needs to cover off in order to maximize the heat deflection. From there, I can then start with designing the mount, will a slide fit be better than a snap fit? How will that effect my tolerances? These are problems that will be more effectively solved through this process if I apply it correctly.

## Problem to Solve

While driving in my car, I have my phone placed on a phone holder that uses a clamp to hold the phone, this is then slide fit onto the driver facing vents on the center console of my car. This works great most times of the year; However, as it gets colder I am starting to use hot air as oppose to cold air in my car for my comfort. This creates a problem for a heat sensitive phone if it is placed on the heating vent. I need to find a way to reduce the effect of the heat from the vent onto my phone.

## Problem Solution

I want to create a heat shield that will be placed in between the phone and the vent. The material used will need to be a good insulator to minimize the heat that escapes to the other side of the shield and heats up my phone. The heat shield itself will not be a challenge to create, the challenge will lay in the design of the fit for the heat shield. There is little space to work with and the phone clamp presents a small surface area to work with in terms of fitting.
