## Research

### Spiral Design Process

The spiral design is a four step repeating process that consists of 1. Planning/Analysis, 2. Risk Evaluation, 3. Development and Testing, 4. Review and Planning Next Iteration. This process will be used in my project to help me focus in on one issue per iteration. For example, I might start with just designing for the area the shield needs to cover off in order to maximize the heat deflection. From there, I can then start with designing the mount, will a slide fit be better than a snap fit? How will that effect my tolerances? These are problems that will be more effectively solved through this process if I apply it correctly.

### FDM Anisotropic

FDM parts are naturally Anisotropic because of how the printing is completed. The printing is done, layer by layer vertically meaning that the forces acting on one axis may cause a different reaction than that of forces acting on a different axis. Because my prototype is going to be loaded vertically, it would be beneficial for me to print my part with the beads moving horizontally, this is because the binding force between the bead layers is not going to be placed under near as much force this way. The mount for the holder can rotate 180 degrees either way. To combat this change in load on the part due to gravity. maybe I could print with diagonal bead layers. This would reduce the overall stress that will occur on the part.

### SLA Shrkinage

SLA shrinkage happens when the resin is undergoing curing. This is because when curing, the resin undergoes a chemical reaction that reduces the overall volume of the print, this is how it hardens. The tolerances will increase as the size increases but is often at around +/-0.1mm-0.2mm for smaller prints. The print will shrink 2-7% overall during curing. This could be the best method for my print due to resin not being nearly as anisotropic as FDM, this would solve the issue of having the print needing to be able to rotate 180% and maintain structural integrity.

### Laser Cut Kerf

Kerf is the width of the material removed from the print due to the heat melting/vaporizing away. Not only is the material removed and therefore altering the fit of the parts. It can also make the surface finishes uneven. Due to fact that the laser cutting kerf opens the tolerances and makes most features larger than intended for. It can make it difficult to calculate the right dimensions for a joint fit. Calculating the kerf offset of the cutter can make help account for the kerf and make snap and slide fits work.

## Problem Definition 

### Phone holder

Phone will overheat when driving in cold weather due to the heater being on. Since the phone holder is clipped to the vent that produces the heat, the phone will be overheated causing my navigation and music to fail.

### Toiletries Holder

I currently have some of my toiletries scattered on my vanity and it unorganized. I could try and create something that could hold or maintain organization within this area.

### Air Tag Holder

I have an air tag in my backpack and car, however in the even that my car would be stolen, the air tag is not hidden well enough to prevent it from being seen and removed. I could create something that would allow me to place the air tag in a more secure and hidden area.
## Problem to Solve

While driving in my car, I have my phone placed on a phone holder that uses a clamp to hold the phone, this is then slide fit onto the driver facing vents on the center console of my car. This works great most times of the year; However, as it gets colder I am starting to use hot air as oppose to cold air in my car for my comfort. This creates a problem for a heat sensitive phone if it is placed on the heating vent. I need to find a way to reduce the effect of the heat from the vent onto my phone.

## Problem Solution

I want to create a heat shield that will be placed in between the phone and the vent. The material used will need to be a good insulator to minimize the heat that escapes to the other side of the shield and heats up my phone. The heat shield itself will not be a challenge to create, the challenge will lay in the design of the fit for the heat shield. There is little space to work with and the phone clamp presents a small surface area to work with in terms of fitting.
