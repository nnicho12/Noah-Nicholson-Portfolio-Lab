# L08 – Design and Print Something Useful

## Problem Statement

This design that I made is to create a place to store toothbrushes on top of my bathroom sink. This is from the fact that I had previously had a cover on the brush head and then stored it directly on the sink surface which is a more unsanitary solution than creating a cup shaped storage unit that will sore the brushes without having the brush head come into contact with anything. I noticed after I had a friend stay and they had to place the brush on the sink with no cover and I thought that there could be a much more sanitary solution to this. My approach was to create a cup that would fit onto the sink edge while having enough structural stability to hold the brush upright without falling over but not high enough to come into contact with the brush head.

## 3D Print

<img width="500" height="275" alt="Screenshot 2026-10-20 120700" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/5ca90a25-1b69-4f5c-8de1-f89c3ae1e79a" />

I used a 20% infill with a rectangular pattern and a wall thickness of 1. I chose these parameters because the height of the print doesn't need to support anything more than a tooth brush so the strength of the print doesn't need to be very height due to the application. I used no supports because there were no points where that would be needed due to the 90 degree nature of the print.

## Lessons Learned

I learned after I created the cup that I could have improved my design by adding in small webbing to the top of the container to separate the brushes from each other. My product successfully removes the brushes from the sink area but in doing so a new potential area of contamination arose from the brushes touching each other. If I were to print this again I would add in some form of this webbing.
