1 - Nord-Lock Washer

<img width="1095" height="564" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/125eba6a-9c95-4d45-a5dc-6bb391bcb1b0" />

  The Nord-Lock washer is a simple but very affective mechanism that is by far the most effective linkage mechanism for screws and washers. The way it works is is by using a two piece washer that has cuts in it at a set angle, if this angle is larger than the angle of the threading of the screw it is on, the mechanism will work. If the screw tries to back itself off, the washer will actually tighten due to the wedge-load effect that is created. This can be used in any industry that requires any sort of construction or building/design.
https://www.nord-lock.com/en-us/nord-lock/original-wedge-locking-washers-product/
