## Analyze

1. Products must be Customizable for the end-user, minimize waste product, and maximize efficiency of assembly, and the proficiency of its designed function. The best practices for Additive Manufacturing are constantly changing so designing in a way that is easily manipulatable is highly valued.

2. FDM is more heat sensitive than SLA printing so to ensure that there is no delamination from heat change mid-print, designers must be extra cautious of the materials used, settings and hardware.
