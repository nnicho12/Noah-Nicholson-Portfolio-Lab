# L02 - Design and Slicing

## Objective

1. Rules for Design for Additive Manufacturing

2. Find one FDM specific consideration

## Analyze

1. Products must be Customizable for the end-user, minimize waste product, and maximize efficiency of assembly, and the proficiency of its designed function. The best practices for Additive Manufacturing are constantly changing so designing in a way that is easily manipulatable is highly valued.

2. FDM is more heat sensitive than SLA printing so to ensure that there is no delamination from heat change mid-print, designers must be extra cautious of the materials used, settings and hardware.

## Decide
_Which geometry did you select, and why? This is your first open design choice in the course — defend it._

## Communicate

1. Topology is a good way to minimize waste in the printing process, and maximize certain aspects of the print based on end-user needs.

2. If the model is set at a 45 degree or more angle, a support is needed in the print to ensure no deformation occurs mid print.
